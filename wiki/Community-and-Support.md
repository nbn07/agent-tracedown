## Using Tracedown?

Tell us in Discussions. Knowing who uses it, and for what, shapes the [Roadmap](Roadmap). With your permission, we'll list your project or company in the README.

## Contributors

Everyone whose pull request is merged is listed in the README's **Contributors** section. Thank you to everyone who has helped so far.

## Code of conduct

Be kind, assume good intent, and keep feedback about the work, not the person. Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that don't.
